MEET OUR EXECUTIVE MANAGERS

Campaign Manager

In my role as the Campaign Manager, I lead the planning, execution, and evaluation of our organization's advocacy and fundraising campaigns. Whether it's raising awareness about pressing health issues, mobilizing support for animal welfare initiatives, or rallying community action for immunization campaigns, I am dedicated to driving meaningful change through strategic campaigns

Health Education Manager

As the Health Education Manager, my primary responsibility is to design and implement comprehensive health education programs aimed at empowering individuals and communities to make informed decisions about their health. Through workshops, seminars, and outreach initiatives, I address a wide range of health topics, including nutrition, hygiene, disease prevention, and mental wellness.

Animal Shelter Manager

As the Animal Shelter Manager, I oversee the day-to-day operations of our shelter facility, ensuring the well-being and care of all animals under our care. My role involves coordinating adoptions, managing volunteer activities, and implementing enrichment programs to promote the physical and emotional health of our animals.
