Frequently Asked Questions

  • How can I volunteer with Wellness Wave?

    Volunteering with Wellness Wave is easy! You can start by filling out our online volunteer application form. Once submitted, our team will review your application and get in touch with you to discuss available opportunities that match your skills and interests.

  • How can I donate to Wellness Wave?

    Donating to Wellness Wave is simple. You can make a one-time donation or set up a recurring donation through our website. Your generous contributions help support our programs and initiatives aimed at improving health outcomes in underserved communities.
