OUR SERVICES

Health Education Programs

Our health education programs are designed to empower children and their families with essential knowledge about maintaining good health. Through interactive workshops and seminars, we cover topics ranging from nutrition and hygiene to disease prevention and mental wellness, ensuring that every child has the tools to lead a healthy life.

Animal Shelter

Our animal shelter provides a safe haven for lost, abandoned, and neglected animals. We offer essential services such as medical care, vaccinations, spaying/neutering, and behavioral training to ensure their well-being. In the past year alone, we've been fortunate to have rescued and rehomed over 200 animals, making a significant impact on the lives of these deserving creatures.

Immunization Campaigns

Vaccinations are one of the most effective ways to protect children from preventable diseases. Our immunization campaigns bring lifesaving vaccines to communities, ensuring that children are fully protected against illnesses like measles, polio, and influenza. By increasing vaccination rates, we help build healthier and more resilient communities.
